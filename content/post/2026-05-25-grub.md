The key to comedy, they say, is timing.  The timing of line breaks can also achieve this.  I found this in a co-worker's wiki page:

```
 ***WARNING***
 grub.conf is the configuration for the "GRand Unified Bootloader"  Screw this
 up, and the machine will need to be reimaged.  
 ***WARNING***
```

I suspect this was intentional given what I know of my co-worker, but since he retired years ago, we may never know for sure.

Side note:  Erich Boleyn and I went to college together.  We were goofing around with one of the alpha releases of [GNU Hurd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Hurd) around 1995.  One day he came in and said:  "I wrote a new bootloader last night!"  That was the beginning of [GRUB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_GRUB#History)!
