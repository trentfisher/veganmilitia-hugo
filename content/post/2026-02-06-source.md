There's a lot of bad genealogy out there, but [this one](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/sources/viewedit/MMZX-K3L) really infuriated me:

> **Web Page (Link to the Record)**
> http://web.cecs.pdx.edu/~trent/fam/people.html
>
> **Where The Record Is Found (Citation)**
> Digital Family Tree
>
> **Describe The Record (Notes)**
> Family Tree of Dan Pidcock. Over 1200 names.

I have no idea who Dan is, or why "1200 names" matters.  These are not Pokemon cards!

But here's the supreme insult:  the url above is *my own web site!*  In the 90's I converted my mother's GEDCOM file into html and put it on the web.  It was crap!  There was not a single source citation in there, I know I mixed up births and christenings, dates and numerous other things.  That GEDCOM was so bad that it was (wisely) rejected by [WeRelate](https://werelate.org), and I determined that fixing it would take so much work, it was best to start over.  So I then entered each person manually, added actual citations, and fixed my stupid mistakes.  After that, I removed all the pages under "fam" and changed it to point back to [WeRelate](https://werelate.org), so those pages haven't been there for well over 10 years.

Update: it just occurred to me who Dan is!  I went and looked at [the old html files](https://web.cecs.pdx.edu/~trent/fam.old/) and found this in the footer of every page:  "Created by Dan Pidcock's Gedcom to HTML converter v1.45".  I am no longer infuriated, I am now laughing.
